Zoning By-law 2026-50 was enacted by City Council on March 11, 2026.

Building permit applications and applications under the Planning Act that are deemed complete on March 11, 2026 or after, must comply with the provisions in Zoning By-law 2008‑250 and the new Zoning By-law 2026‑50, with the most restrictive provisions from both by-laws applying.

Building permit applications that are deemed complete before March 11, 2026 are subject to the provisions of Zoning By-law 2008-250.

On July 2, 2026 the OLT made an oral decision ordering that the parts of Zoning By-law 2026-50, not included in the 25 appeals, may come into force. This decision is retroactive to March 11, 2026, the date of enactment of the new Zoning By-law. As a result of this oral decision, the majority of Zoning By-law 2026-50 is now in force. Please see the memo to Council for more information.

The text of Zoning By-law 2026-50 on ottawa.ca will be updated as soon as possible to indicate which provisions remain under appeal, shown in bold, italic font. For site-specific appeals, an *_ will be added to the Zoning Map to indicate the zone code under appeal (ex. *_CM1). Until these updates are completed, please refer to the memo linked above for information about the provisions that are not in force.

This online consolidation of Zoning By-law 2026-50 is updated following every Council meeting where zoning by-law amendments are enacted. The consolidation date on the landing page of Zoning By-law 2026-50 is the date the consolidation was last revised to reflect decisions made by Council.

Part 19 - Amendment Schedule to the Zoning By-law, includes a list of by-laws enacted by Council that amend Zoning By-law 2026-50. This includes the amending by-law numbers, the by-law title, the status of each amendment, the date the by-law was enacted by Council and links to the related Committee Reports and Council decisions. If relevant, it also includes the Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT) order number and date, and additional notes. The provisions of each amending by-law are incorporated into the text and Zoning Map as pending amendments which are displayed as follows: 

  • If the text of the Zoning By-law is affected by an amending by-law, the affected text is shown in bold, italic font.  When the pending amendment comes into effect, the bold italics are removed from the text of the consolidation.
  • If a zone code on the Zoning Map is affected by an amending by-law, an asterisk and an underscore (*_) is added at the beginning of the zone code and the zone code is changed to show the proposed zone code. When the pending amendment comes into effect, the asterisk and underscore are removed from the zone code on the Zoning Map. 

An amendment comes into full force and effect when the appeal period of the amending by-law has expired and no appeals against the by-law have been received, or appeals were received and have either been withdrawn or disposed of by the OLT.

Please include the subject address, proposed use, and other pertinent information relevant to the inquiry.  Upon receipt of the inquiry, a phone call or an email response will be provided within 2 to 3 business days.
